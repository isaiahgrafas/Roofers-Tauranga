What We Do

Every roof.
Built to last.

We specialise in long run iron roofing — New Zealand's most durable and weather-resistant roofing option. Whether you need a full re-roof, a new build installation, or targeted repairs, we bring the same LBP-standard workmanship to every job.

01
Roof Replacements

Complete re-roofing using premium long run iron. We remove your old roof, inspect the structure, replace any damaged timber, and install a new roof that will last decades. Scaffolding and clean-up included.

02
New Installations

New roof installations for new builds and additions across Tauranga and the Bay of Plenty. We work directly with builders and project managers to keep your timeline on track.

03
Roof Repairs

Leaks, storm damage, loose flashings, failed sealants — we diagnose and fix correctly. No unnecessary upselling. If a repair will do the job, we'll tell you.

04
Roof Reports

Detailed site assessment with roof plan and photos — ideal for pre-purchase inspections or insurance purposes. Delivered as a full written report. $300 + GST.

05
Google Earth Estimates

Get a free indicative estimate without a site visit. We use satellite imagery to assess your roof size and condition and provide a ballpark figure — completely free.

06
Commercial Roofing

Commercial and light industrial roofing projects across the Bay of Plenty. We handle large-scale re-roofing with minimal disruption and full compliance with NZ building standards.

Roofers Tauranga is a family owned and operated roofing company working in association with Artisans Roofing. With 5 years operating and 10+ years of industry experience, we've built a reputation across the Bay of Plenty for quality installation, durable products and workmanship that lasts.

We are Licensed Building Practitioners — a legal requirement for any work that affects the weather-tightness of your home. This protects you as a homeowner and ensures everything is done to the required standard.

We use only quality materials that are tested to NZ standards and rated for our coastal climate. A roof is your home's first line of defence — we treat it that way.

Free quotes are available via site visit or Google Earth estimate. We're transparent on pricing from the start — no surprises when the invoice arrives.
